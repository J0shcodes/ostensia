# Ostensia: Evidence Register (mid-build validation, report 05)

**Status:** research artefact, not a project decision.
**Research date:** 2026-11-05. Every source below was accessed on 2026-11-05.
**Researcher:** Claude Code (model `claude-opus-5-5`), at the developer's request. This
is desk research. It includes no interviews and no customer validation.

## How to use this register

- Reports 01 to 04 cite the IDs below, for example [EV-07] or [SA-03].
- **SA-** entries are statements from the project's own self-audit (Report 00,
  `docs/research/mid-build-validation/00-implementation-baseline.md`, commit
  `9c4293b`). They describe what Ostensia currently does. They are **self-audit,
  not independent evidence**, and are never used as evidence of demand, competitor
  capability or PayPal behaviour.
- **PR-** entries are other project records (developer-maintained, self-reported).
- **EV-** entries are external sources.
- **"Read in full"** means the page itself was fetched and read. **"Search excerpt"**
  means only a search-engine summary was seen, because the page was not opened or
  returned an error. Search excerpts carry lower confidence by default.
- **Confidence** applies to the claim as recorded, not to the source in general:
  - **High:** an official or primary source, read in full, stating the claim directly.
  - **Medium:** a primary source seen only in excerpt, a credible secondary source,
    or a vendor describing its own product.
  - **Low:** a single secondary source, aggregators, conflicting reports, or an
    unidentified page.
